Audio is analysed inside this page and never sent anywhere. Nothing is recorded, stored, or transcribed — the meter only measures how loudness rises and falls over time, which is enough to count syllables and silences. Pace is an estimate from that envelope, so treat it as a tendency, not a stopwatch.
